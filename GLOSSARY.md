# Resume Preparation

A candidate's professional history and the application documents derived from it.

## Language

**Profile**:
The candidate's complete, reviewed professional history and qualifications, independent of any particular job application.
_Avoid_: Resume, application

**Resume**:
A concise selection of the candidate's factual experience and qualifications presented for a job application.
_Avoid_: Full profile, complete history

**Job Description**:
The employer's description of a role, its requirements, and its desired qualifications.
_Avoid_: Candidate profile

**Match Report**:
An assessment of how the profile supports a job description, including a percentage estimate, supporting evidence, and gaps. The percentage is not a probability of hiring or passing an employer's screening.
_Avoid_: Hiring probability, ATS approval score

**Cover Letter**:
A concise letter connecting the candidate's factual experience and qualifications to a particular role and organization.
_Avoid_: Resume summary

**PDF Readability Check**:
An inspection of whether a resume PDF exposes readable text, a plausible reading order, and recognizable document details. It does not certify compatibility with an employer's applicant tracking system.
_Avoid_: ATS approval, hiring prediction

**Evidence Coverage Percentage**:
A weighted estimate of how strongly the documented profile supports the requirements identified in a job description. It is part of the match report, not a measure of the candidate's undocumented abilities.
_Avoid_: Hiring probability, interview probability
